How it works. ZeoPlant is a natural zeolite blended into the planting soil. Its porous crystalline structure stores water and nutrients in the root zone and releases them back to the plant, cutting irrigation demand. Enter your project size, blend ratios and prices below — the calculator returns the material quantities you need, the water & cost you save, and the full ROI / payback, using the same formulas as the source spreadsheet.

Technical Reference

Soil texture & methodology

USDA soil-texture triangle

Target blend sits in the Sand / Sandy Loam region — high infiltration with improved retention once ZeoPlant is introduced.

Formulas used

  • Mixture volume = area × mixing depth
    m³ = area(m²) × depth(m)
  • Component mass = (volume × ratio%) × bulk density
  • New IWR = baseline IWR × (1 − saving%) ; saved = baseline − new
  • Season demand = area × IWR × factor% × days ⁄ 1000 → m³
  • Annual cost = m³ × water price ; saving = cost₀ − cost₁
  • Investment = ZeoPlant kg × price ; payback = investment ⁄ annual saving ; ROI = 1 ⁄ payback
